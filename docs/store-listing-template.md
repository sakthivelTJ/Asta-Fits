## Full Description (Google Play & Apple App Store)

"My Magic is Never Giving Up!"

Unleash your inner Magic Knight with **Asta's Fit**, the ultimate anime-inspired fitness RPG designed to make physical workouts as thrilling as leveling up in an anime! 

Whether you're starting from zero or striving to reach Devil Union strength, Asta's Fit transforms push-ups, squats, planks, and pull-ups into anti-magic daily quests.

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES:

⚔️ **SURPASS YOUR LIMITS PROGRESSION**
Complete daily workout quests tailored to your goal and equipment. Earn XP, rank up from Magicless Novice (Rank E) to Senior Magic Knight (Rank B) and Vice Captain (Rank A)!

🤖 **ON-DEVICE CAMERA REP COUNTING**
Position your phone camera and let on-device AI count your reps automatically while checking your movement depth. 100% private — video never leaves your phone!

🔥 **STREAK & WILLPOWER DISCIPLINE**
Maintain your daily training streak to unlock rank trials. Rest days and protective shields prevent overtraining and burnouts.

🏆 **SQUAD LEADERBOARDS**
Exchange friend codes with fellow trainees. Compare weekly XP gains on the squad leaderboard and motivate each other to train harder!

🥗 **HUNTER DIET & VITALITY**
Receive custom daily calorie and macro targets based on your physique archetype. Connect health data for daily Vitality stat boosts!

🔒 **100% PRIVATE & AD-FREE**
No ads, no data selling. Your privacy is paramount.

## Marketing Claims to Avoid (Strict Policy Compliance)
> [!CAUTION]
> **DO NOT INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING CLAIMS IN STORE LISTINGS OR ADS:**
> 1. Medical or health diagnosis claims ("Cures obesity", "Guaranteed 10kg weight loss in 30 days").
> 2. Direct anime copyright/trademark claims ("Official Black Clover app", "Play as Asta from Shonen Jump").
> 3. Unrealistic physical promises ("Instant 6-pack abs without dieting").
> 4. Diagnostic medical claims regarding camera ML pose tracking ("Replaces physical therapy").
