# Privacy Policy for Asta's Fit

**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

Asta's Fit ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
When you create an account or sign in, we collect:
- Display name and email address
- Authentication credentials (managed securely via Firebase Authentication or Sign in with Apple)

### B. Fitness & Progression Profile
To calculate personalized workouts and XP rewards, we store:
- Physique goals, starting fitness experience, available equipment, and physical limitations
- Workout logs (exercise sets, completed reps, weights, duration)
- Level, rank, XP, and streak progress

### C. Health & Vitality Data (Optional)
If you grant permission to Apple Health or Google Health Connect:
- Daily step count and sleep duration
- **Note:** Only raw daily totals leave your phone. No detailed timestamped health samples are uploaded.

### D. On-Device Camera Rep Counting (100% Private)
- If you use camera-assisted rep counting, pose detection is performed **entirely on your device** in real time.
- **Camera frames, images, and video are NEVER recorded, saved, stored, or transmitted over the internet.**

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to:
- Provide personalized daily fitness quests and track level progression
- Display your rank on weekly squad leaderboards (unless disabled in settings)
- Send daily quest reminders and streak warnings (if notifications are enabled)
- Prevent cheating and maintain progression balancing

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do **NOT** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers. We use trusted infrastructure providers:
- **Firebase (Google Cloud):** Database, authentication, Cloud Functions, analytics, and crash reporting.
- **Apple Health / Google Health Connect:** Read-only access to step/sleep counts with explicit user consent.

---

## 4. Leaderboard & Squad Privacy

- You can control whether your profile appears on friends' leaderboards via **Settings -> Weekly Leaderboard Visibility**.
- When turned off, your rank, level, and weekly XP are completely hidden from other users.

---

## 5. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:
- **In-App:** Go to **Settings -> Account Actions -> Delete Account & Data** and type "DELETE".
- **Web Request:** Visit [https://astasfit.app/delete-account](https://astasfit.app/delete-account) to submit a deletion request.

Upon deletion, your profile, workout logs, health records, friend connections, and friend code are permanently purged from our servers.
